Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

The short version: Shelfie does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account and no server. Everything you add stays on your iPhone or iPad.

Who we are

Shelfie (“the app”) is a reading tracker for iPhone and iPad made by Christian Tania, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app and this website.

What the app stores, and where

To work, Shelfie saves the following only on your device:

  • Your books, categories, reading sessions, reading streaks and notes.
  • Your settings, such as your reminder time and room colors.
  • Whether Shelfie Pro is active (Lifetime or Monthly), so the app can open without checking every time.

We never receive this information, and we can’t see it.

What we don’t collect

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No servers of our own

Shelfie contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking, and it does not use your data to track you across other apps or websites. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Notifications

Reading reminders and streak alerts are local notifications: your device schedules them itself. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else to deliver them. You can turn them off in the app’s Settings, or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Shelfie.

Sharing your shelf and saving images

When you use Share your shelf, the picture is created on your device. It goes only where you choose to send it, and the service you share it with (for example Instagram or Messages) handles it under its own privacy policy.

If you choose Save Image, iOS asks for permission to add photos to your library. This is add-only: Shelfie can’t see or read any photos in your library.

Purchases

Shelfie Pro, both the one-time Lifetime purchase and the Monthly subscription, is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes every payment and renewal; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that Pro is active, so it can turn on the extra features. Apple’s handling of your purchase is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone or iPad with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS may include Shelfie’s data in that backup, under the settings you chose with Apple. We don’t have access to your backups.

Deleting your data

You can delete any book, note, session or category in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. Because we don’t keep a copy, there’s nothing else to delete.

Children

Shelfie is suitable for readers of all ages and doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If a change ever meant collecting data, we would ask for your permission in the app first.
