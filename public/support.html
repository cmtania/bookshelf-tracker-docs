Getting started

How do I add a book?

On the Bookshelf tab, tap a compartment (or its name in the row at the bottom) to zoom in, then tap + at the top. Enter the title, author and number of pages, pick a status and a spine color, and tap Save. The book goes into the compartment you zoomed into.

How do I open a book?

Zoom into its compartment and tap its spine, or tap its name in the row at the bottom. The book slides off the shelf and turns to show its cover, with Log reading and Edit underneath. Drag left or right to turn it around, and tap anywhere else to put it back.

How do I log what I read?

Open the book and tap Log reading, then Log reading again. Enter the page you’re on, or switch to Pages read and enter how many pages you read. You can add the minutes you spent and change the date. When you reach the last page, Shelfie offers to mark the book as finished.

How do I edit or delete a book?

Open the book and tap Edit. You can change any detail there, including its category and current page. Delete book at the bottom removes the book together with its notes and reading sessions.

How do I add notes?

Open the book, tap Log reading and scroll to Notes. Tap Add note, write your note and, if you like, the page it’s about. Tap a note to edit it, or swipe left to delete it.

Streaks and reminders

How is a streak counted?

A streak is the number of days in a row with at least one logged reading session. Every book has its own streak, each category has one for all its books, and there’s an overall streak for all your reading. Days follow your iPhone’s time zone.

Why did my streak reset?

A streak breaks when a whole day passes without a reading session. If you haven’t read yet today, your streak is still safe until midnight. Only logged sessions count, so remember to tap Log reading after you read. You can also log a session for an earlier time with the When field.

What is the “streak at risk” alert?

At 8 PM, Shelfie reminds you if a book you’re reading has a streak going but no reading logged that day. It never sends this alert on days you’ve already read. You can turn it off in Settings → Reminders.

My reminders don’t arrive. What can I do?

Check these, in order:

  • In iOS Settings → Notifications → Shelfie, Allow Notifications is on.
  • In Shelfie’s Settings, Daily reminder is on and the time is right.
  • The book is marked Reading, and Reminders for this book is on in its Edit screen.
  • A Focus mode, such as Sleep, isn’t silencing notifications at that time.

Open Shelfie once after changing a setting, so it can schedule the next reminders.

Shelf and categories

How do I add, rename or reorder categories?

Use the Categories tab. Tap + to add one, tap a category and then Edit to rename it or change its color, and tap Edit in the list to drag categories into a new order. Each category is one compartment of the bookcase, filled from the top left. You can also rename a category from the Bookshelf tab: zoom into it and tap the pencil.

How many categories can I have?

The bookcase has 10 compartments, so up to 10 categories. The free version includes 3, and Shelfie Pro opens all 10.

Why can’t I delete a category?

So that you never lose books by accident, a category can only be deleted when it’s empty. Move its books to another category (in each book’s Edit screen) or delete them first.

Why are some books lying flat?

Books marked Want to read lie in a pile at the right end of their shelf, just like a to-read stack at home. When a shelf is full, extra books join that pile too. If even the pile is full, the shelf’s label shows how many more there are, like “Novels · +3”. You can always see every book in the Categories tab.

Calendar

How do I see the whole month?

The Calendar tab opens on the current week. Tap its title, tap the small handle under the days, or swipe down on the calendar to see the whole month. Swipe up or tap again to go back to the week. The arrows move one week or one month at a time.

What do the colored dots mean?

Each dot is a book you read that day, in the book’s spine color. Tap a day to see every session: the book, the pages you read and the minutes, if you logged them.

Shelfie Pro and purchases

What does Shelfie Pro include?

Unlimited books, all 10 bookcase compartments, and room colors for the bookcase, walls and floor, including the premium finishes and floor patterns. You can get Pro two ways: Pro Monthly, a subscription you can cancel anytime, or Pro Lifetime, a one-time purchase. Both include exactly the same features.

Should I choose Monthly or Lifetime?

If you’d like to try Pro first, start with Monthly. If you know you’ll keep reading with Shelfie, Lifetime is the better deal: it costs about as much as a few months of Monthly, and you never pay again. The paywall shows exactly how many months it takes to pay for itself.

How do I cancel Pro Monthly?

In Shelfie, go to Settings and tap Manage subscription. You can also open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and Shelfie. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all subscription billing.

What happens when my subscription ends?

Pro stays on until the end of the month you paid for. After that, your books, notes and reading history all stay on your shelf, and you can keep reading, logging and editing them. You won’t be able to add books or categories beyond the free limits, and the room goes back to its default colors until you subscribe again or get Lifetime.

I’m on Monthly. How do I switch to Lifetime?

Go to Settings and tap Switch to Lifetime. After you buy it, Shelfie reminds you to cancel the monthly subscription, because Apple keeps renewing it until you do. Tap Manage subscription to cancel it. Pro stays on either way.

I bought Pro but it isn’t active. What do I do?

Go to Settings in Shelfie and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. This also brings Pro to a new iPhone.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Shelfie purchase.

Room colors and sharing

How do I change the room’s colors?

With Shelfie Pro, tap the paintbrush on the Bookshelf tab (when you’re zoomed out), or go to Settings → Room colors. Choose a bookcase, wall and floor. The room changes live behind the picker. Reset to default colors brings back the original look.

How do I share my shelf?

On the Bookshelf tab, zoomed out, tap the share button. Shelfie makes a picture of your bookcase with your book count and streak. Tap Share to post it or send it. If you choose Save Image, iOS asks once for permission to add photos to your library.

Your data

Where is my library stored?

Only on your iPhone. Shelfie has no account and no server, so we never see your books, notes or reading history. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I move my library to a new iPhone?

Shelfie’s data is included in your regular iPhone backup. Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start, and your library comes along. Then tap Restore purchase in Settings to bring back Shelfie Pro.

How do I delete all my data?

In Shelfie, go to Settings → Reset all data. That deletes every book, category, note and reading session and gives you a fresh bookcase, with the welcome screens again. Your Shelfie Pro purchase stays. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
